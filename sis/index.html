Singapore Indoor Stadium · BABYMONSTER [CHOOM] — unofficial concept

An independent fan model of the South Stage layout for the 2026 BABYMONSTER World Tour [CHOOM] at Singapore Indoor Stadium (28–29 Nov 2026). Not an official seat map, view guarantee or booking tool; check the official ticketing site before you buy.

From the official seat plan: section list and ticket categories with prices (excluding booking fees); stage at the south end with a T-runway; Standing Pen B (west) and Pen A (east), each VIP Standing in front of CAT 1 Standing; VIP Seated blocks PA1–PA3 with CAT 2 floor seats behind; FOH north of the PA blocks; Level 2 sections 208–234 and Level 3 sections 308–314, 318–320, 322–324, 328–334 (no 321, no 100-level — the retractable seats are stowed). 208 / 234 (CAT 7) and 308 / 334 (CAT 8) are sold as restricted view. The official plan is marked "not drawn to scale", so the floor layout here is an approximation.

From the public Pacifa seat-view data: the plan shape of every Level 2/3 section (re-projected from a perspective aerial image, so expect a few metres of error), continuous row numbering (Level 2 rows 13–27, Level 3 rows 28–36), and published seat counts for 208, 210, 214, 220, 224, 231, 234, 308, 310, 314, 322, 328 and 334. Other sections' seat counts are fitted to their outline.

Building: Kenzo Tange's 1989 pagoda roof (diamond plan ~200 × 120 m, 45 m high, ~39 m to the ceiling) with the stainless space-frame and central light trough; dark ceiling, purple seats with maroon accent blocks, blue Level 3 fascia, after the interior photo you supplied. Rake (Level 2 ≈ 0.40 m rise per row, Level 3 ≈ 0.50 m) is estimated from published cross-sections and tuned by eye against the official view-from-seat images for 220 and 322.

Assumed: stage size, LED screens, flown side screens, lighting rig and speaker hangs (the production design is unpublished — this is a generic concert set); pen and floor block sizes; floor row/seat numbering (rows from the stage, seat 1 at the west end); seat numbering direction in the stands; which blocks carry maroon seats. Photos and the official plan image are not embedded.
