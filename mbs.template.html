Sands Theatre, an unofficial seat plan

Drawn from the official Grand Theatre seating plan published by Marina Bay Sands, which matches the three level hall sold today as the Sands Theatre. That match is inferred from the plan and seat counts, not from an official rename notice.

This plan shows 2,155 seats marked on that plan: 1,238 in the Stalls (71 on the orchestra pit lift in rows A to C), 177 in the Dress Circle (boxes 1 to 6), and 740 in the Grand Circle (boxes 7 and 8). The published capacity is 2,173, so the drawing is 18 short. No seats were invented to close the gap.

Row letters and counts follow the plan. Rows I and II are not used, and Dress Circle row D gaps are shown as wheelchair spaces. Seat numbers here run left to right across each row as a guide and may not match the tickets. The curve of the rows, the aisle widths and box positions are approximate.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Marina Bay Sands or BASE Entertainment Asia. No live availability and no booking.
