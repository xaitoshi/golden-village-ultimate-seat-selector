Starbucks at Bugis Junction, an unofficial concept

An independent fan model of the Starbucks cafe at Bugis Junction, Level 1, unit 01 106, Victoria Street, Singapore. Not an official Starbucks product, store map or booking tool, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Starbucks, Maxim's Caterers, CapitaLand or Bugis Junction. There is no Starbucks logo artwork in this model, only simple text.

Layout: a corner unit with black framed glass along two sides, opening onto a covered arcade terrace. The terrace has a dark timber beamed ceiling, ceiling fans, a stone tiled floor, black metal bistro chairs and small round dark tables. Inside: a light wood plank floor, a long wood grain wall on the right, a dark ceiling with black track lighting, olive armchairs around small round white marble tables along the wall and windows, a long communal wood table with black chairs near the window, a merch shelf and a grab and go fridge mid store, and the service counter with menu boards and a pastry case at the back. Geometry is estimated from photos and is not a measured survey.

Hours for the slider: 7:30 am to 10:00 pm on weekdays, 7:30 am to midnight on weekends, following the Bugis Junction mall listing (Friday and Saturday may close at midnight). The CapitaLand directory lists 7:30 am to 10:00 pm. Published hours disagree slightly across listings, so treat this window as an approximation.

Crowd: occupancy is a simulation for exploring the room, not live data from the store. The daily curve is a guess (morning commute, lunch peak, afternoon study crowd, evening dip), with indoor armchairs and window seats filling first and the terrace filling later or in the evening. The same hour always shows the same taken seats.

Sources: CapitaLand Bugis Junction store directory · Bugis Junction mall store listing · owner supplied interior photos used only as a layout guide and not shown on this site.
