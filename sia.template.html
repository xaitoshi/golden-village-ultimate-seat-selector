Singapore Airlines-style 2027 Business — unofficial concept

An independent fan model of the next-generation long-haul Business Class Singapore Airlines unveiled on 6 Nov 2026 (designed by Designworks, built by RECARO; due from 2H 2027 on retrofitted A350-900 long-haul/ULR and new 777-9). Not an official Singapore Airlines product, seat map or booking tool.

From published reports: 1-2-1 layout with direct aisle access; sliding doors at every suite; 52 in shell/door height; 50 in pitch; 22 in seat width (A350); 22 in 4K OLED screen (A350); window suites alternating window-aligned / aisle-aligned; lowerable electronic divider so selected centre pairs become companion beds; 42 Business seats on the A350 long-haul; speckled side console, wireless charging, stowed bi-fold table under the screen.

Assumed: row numbers reuse today's A350-900 long-haul Business map (rows 11–17 forward, 18–22 rear, no row 13, row 18 centre pair only) because the 2027 seat map hasn't been published; which rows are companion pairs; exact suite footprint, console sizes and positions; cabin colours and trim (modelled on the renders: charcoal shells, bronze trim, light grey cushions); the bulkhead bird/arrow artwork is an abstract impression, not the airline's logo. Screen content is a placeholder.

Sources: MainlyMiles · The Points Guy · current A350 LH seat map.
